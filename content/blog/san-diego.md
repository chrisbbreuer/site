The plan was 100 miles in San Diego over four days. Plans changed a little. I came home two days early, and I have been making up the difference at TriFit in Santa Monica since.

The two days I did get were good ones: 65 miles between them, most of it along the coast. And I count all of it. The miles before a run, the ones the watch missed, the treadmill at the hotel: they flow into one another, so the numbers here are the day, not just the GPS file.

## Day one: downtown, La Jolla, and back

Before the run started, I ran 5.4 miles through downtown, because I wanted to see more of it. It was not yet 7 AM, the city was still quiet, and the legs were happy at 8:48 a mile.

Then the run itself: out past Old Town and around Mission Bay, up the coast through Pacific Beach to La Jolla, a loop up onto the mesa through UC San Diego, and back down into the city. The watch recorded 32.4 miles of it. Two more went untracked along the way, at about 9:20 a mile.

![A Wildloop activity card for day one: 39.77 miles, 7:36:54 moving, 11:29 per mile and 1,296 ft, with the route drawn on a dark map of San Diego from downtown up the coast to Torrey Pines and back](/images/blog/san-diego/wildloop-day-one-lg.avif "Day one, warmup and untracked miles included")

At La Jolla Cove the marine layer was still sitting on the water, and the sea lions were doing what sea lions do at ten in the morning, which is nothing, in a pile.

![Sea lions asleep in a pile on pale sandstone above the surf at La Jolla Cove, under a grey marine layer](/images/blog/san-diego/img-6564-lg.avif "Mid-morning, La Jolla Cove")

Then UC San Diego. I knew it was a big campus. I did not know it was this big. You run into it expecting to be out the other side in a few minutes, and it keeps going: buildings, roads, paths, and then more of all three.

The only real problem all day, besides a small blister, was chafing. It bothered me a lot between miles 30 and 40. That one is on me: heading out for a day that long without Vaseline ready is a rookie mistake, and not one I plan to make twice.

![A turtle hauled out on a log in a reedy pond, sunning itself in bright midday light](/images/blog/san-diego/img-6576-lg.avif "Somebody who had the sun figured out")

## Day two: a marathon, to a beach

The plan for Friday was about a marathon. I wanted to finish at a nice beach rather than run a loop, and Mission Beach was about 15 scenic miles from where I was staying, up near Del Mar. That is not a marathon, so the rest came first: 10 miles on the treadmill in the hotel gym at 9 minutes a mile, a 30 minute meeting, and then out the door.

![A Wildloop activity card for the treadmill run in the hotel gym: 10.00 miles, 1:30:00 moving, 9:00 per mile and an average heart rate of 136, with ten even splits of 9:00 where the map would be](/images/blog/san-diego/wildloop-treadmill-lg.avif "Ten miles, ten splits, no view")

It was hot. I left at 1:20 in the afternoon and went straight down to Torrey Pines State Beach with the sun almost overhead.

![The coast path at Torrey Pines State Beach, a railing running ahead toward the bluffs, surf on the right and the sun high in a deep blue sky](/images/blog/san-diego/img-6592-lg.webp "1:45 PM, Torrey Pines State Beach")

From there it was up the Torrey Pines grade, the high point of the day at about 450 ft, past UC San Diego again, which is still huge, then down through La Jolla Shores and La Jolla Cove, along Bird Rock and Pacific Beach, and onto the sand at Mission Beach.

![Mission Beach in late afternoon light, wide sand with tracks across it, a few people at the waterline and La Jolla on the hill in the distance](/images/blog/san-diego/img-6610-lg.avif "4:50 PM, Mission Beach")

![A Wildloop activity card for day two: 25.27 miles, 4:02:17 moving, 9:35 per mile and 1,007 ft, with the route drawn on a dark map from Del Mar down the coast past Torrey Pines and La Jolla to Mission Beach](/images/blog/san-diego/wildloop-day-two-lg.avif "Day two, hotel treadmill included")

Then dinner, and the Padres doing their part.

![A plate of pasta with peas on an orange and white striped bar, the Padres up 4 to 1 on the Cubs in the Wild Card on the TV above](/images/blog/san-diego/img-6612-lg.avif "Day two, done")

## What I learned

Sun protection is very important. Not a nice-to-have, not something for the beach day after. Hours on an exposed coast in October are their own kind of load, and the sun takes something out of you whether or not you notice it happening. Sunscreen before, sunscreen again during, a hat, glasses. The turtle had it right.

UC San Diego is huge. Plan your route through it, and plan for it to take longer than it looks.

And carry Vaseline.

## The totals

- **Day one:** 39.8 miles. 5.4 through downtown before the start, 32.4 on the watch, 2 it missed. 7:37 moving, 11:29 a mile, 1,296 ft of climbing.
- **Day two:** 25.3 miles. 10 on the hotel treadmill at an average heart rate of 136, then 15.3 to Mission Beach. 4:02 moving, 9:35 a mile, 1,007 ft.
- **Both:** 65 miles in 11:39 of moving time.

The other 35 are happening at TriFit, one session at a time.
